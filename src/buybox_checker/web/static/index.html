      How we know who wins

      1. We open the product page as a shopper in each state, with one zipcode per state.
      2. The buybox is the panel with the Add to Cart button. The seller under "Sold by" there is the winner, and its price is the buybox price.
      3. No Add to Cart button means nobody holds the buybox in that state.
